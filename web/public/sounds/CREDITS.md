# Night sounds

Three field recordings from [Freesound](https://freesound.org), each dedicated to the public domain under [Creative Commons 0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Two are Queensland nights. The licences were read on each sound's own page (checked 26 Nov 2026). Thanks to the recordists all the same.

| File | Recording | By | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| `night.m4a` | [NightSounds2](https://freesound.org/people/tonant/sounds/202934/): "a windy night of a rainforest gully in southern Queensland" | tonant | Gold Coast, Queensland |
| `night-busy.m4a` | [Frog Chorus](https://freesound.org/people/Downrigger/sounds/269420/): "thousands of frogs singing in anticipation of rain" | Downrigger | Glass House Mountains, Queensland |
| `campfire.m4a` | [Campfire (Position 2)](https://freesound.org/people/SKrafft/sounds/681367/) | SKrafft | not stated |

Made from Freesound's high-quality previews with ffmpeg:
- `night` is 32.5 s from 6:30, the calmest stretch (it skips a gust or plane at 5:24–6:14). 32.5 s puts the seam between matching parts of its insects' two-second pulse, with a 3 s equal-power crossfade.
- `night-busy` is 24 s from 0:01, skipping the recording's fade-in and fade-out, with a 3 s crossfade.
- `campfire` is 18 s from the start, with a 2 s crossfade.
- All three are mono, loudness-normalised to −24 LUFS, AAC at 48 kbps.

Earlier versions used crickets from Canada and Mexico, before these Queensland recordings were found.
