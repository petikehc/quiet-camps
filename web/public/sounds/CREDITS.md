# Night sounds

Three field recordings from [Freesound](https://freesound.org), each dedicated to the public domain under [Creative Commons 0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The licence was read on each sound's own page (checked 24 Nov 2026). Thanks to the recordists all the same.

| File | Recording | By | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| `night.m4a` | [180808 Crickets night insects busy chorus NOTL](https://freesound.org/people/TRP/sounds/571079/) | TRP | Niagara-on-the-Lake, Canada |
| `night-busy.m4a` | [Crickets during the night, close to the beach in Mexico](https://freesound.org/people/felix.blume/sounds/479041/) | felix.blume | Chacahua lagoon, Oaxaca, Mexico |
| `campfire.m4a` | [Campfire (Position 2)](https://freesound.org/people/SKrafft/sounds/681367/) | SKrafft | not stated |

Made from Freesound's high-quality previews with ffmpeg:
- `night` is 32 s from 1:00, and `night-busy` 32 s from 2:00; each has a 3 s equal-power crossfade at the seam.
- `campfire` is 18 s from the start, with a 2 s crossfade.
- All three are mono, loudness-normalised to −24 LUFS, AAC at 48 kbps.

None is Australian: no public-domain recording of a Queensland night was found on Wikimedia Commons or Freesound. A local recording would be a welcome swap.
